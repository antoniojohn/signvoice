Privacy

SignVoice is a personal project, not a commercial product. Your webcam video never leaves your browser — hand landmarks are extracted locally, and only those landmark numbers (never video frames or images) are sent to the server for matching. Registered signs, samples, and account details are stored in a local database on whichever machine runs this app. Nothing here is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Terms of use

This project is provided as-is, for demonstration and educational purposes. There's no uptime guarantee, and features may change, move, or break as the project keeps evolving. Use it at your own discretion.
